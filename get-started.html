01 · Import

Bring in a supported financial export instead of typing every account by hand. Free and Pro support compatible imports, including supported Fidelity Full View and Positions formats. You can also explore fictional sample data first.

02 · Review and fill gaps

Check parsed values and dates before relying on them. Add or correct details the file did not provide; import support depends on the file format.

03 · Back up

Download a backup of your local records and keep it somewhere safe. Local browsers do not sync automatically.
