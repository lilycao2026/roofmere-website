For first-time users · Getting started

Start with a few balances—not your entire financial life.

You don't need to enter your entire financial life on day one. Start with an account, a property or a debt, then build a clearer picture at your own pace.

01 · Add

Enter a few balances or try the fictional sample household first. Imports depend on your edition and supported formats.

02 · Review

Check which numbers are estimates, what you owe and which information is missing or outdated.

03 · Back up

Download a backup of your local records and keep it somewhere safe. Local browsers do not sync automatically.
