Vision

Your Personal Data Layer.

Your data stays yours. You decide who gets access.

AI is changing the way we work, learn, and manage our lives. But as technology becomes more powerful, one question matters more than ever: Who controls the personal information that makes AI useful?

At Roofmere, the answer is simple: you do.

Starting with your financial life

Today, Roofmere is a privacy-first Household Financial Organizer.

It helps you bring accounts, investments, properties, debts, and important financial knowledge into one place—without requiring you to connect your bank accounts or track every purchase.

The goal is simple: spend less time searching for information and more time understanding what matters.

More than data. Knowledge that stays with you.

A balance alone doesn't tell the whole story.

Your financial knowledge includes documents, history, context, where information came from, and when it was last updated.

Roofmere's long-term vision is to help you build a personal data foundation that preserves this knowledge—so you don't have to start over every time you change tools.

Your AI can change. Your data stays yours.

Imagine a future where your personal information isn't locked inside one app, one AI assistant, or one company's ecosystem.

Whether you choose ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, a local AI model, or tools that haven't been invented yet, your data foundation remains under your control.

You decide:
  • What information to share
  • Which tools can receive it
  • What details to leave out
  • Whether to share anything at all

Once information is shared with an external service, it may not be possible to take it back. That's why meaningful control starts before sharing—not after.

AI should work with your data on your terms—not the other way around.

You hold the keys.

Think of your personal data as a home you own.

Your financial records, documents, history, and personal knowledge belong inside.

AI assistants and apps may help you use that information, but they shouldn't automatically have the keys to everything.

You decide which doors to open and what information can pass through them.

Your data. Your door. Your rules.

Built for today. Designed with tomorrow in mind.

Roofmere starts with a practical financial organizer you can use today.

The broader Personal Data Layer, connections to different AI tools, and granular permission controls represent a long-term direction—not features currently available.

The goal is not to build another platform that owns your information.

It's to help create a future where your information works for you, wherever technology goes next.

Your wealth. Your knowledge. Your future.
