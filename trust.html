Roofmere   /   Privacy, Trust & Security

Your financial information. Your choice of where it lives.

Roofmere helps you organize household accounts, investments, properties, and debts without requiring a bank connection or daily expense tracking. The Local and Self-Hosted Cloud editions have different data-storage and security responsibilities.

How your data is handled

Local — stored in your browser

Local financial records are stored in your browser using IndexedDB, rather than in a Roofmere-hosted financial database. You enter or import information yourself. Your browser and device control access to that local storage; clearing site data or losing the device may remove it.

Self-Hosted Cloud — stored in services you configure

The Self-Hosted Cloud edition uses a deployment and database configured by the customer, rather than the Local browser-only storage model. Authentication, database access controls, service configuration, and backups matter in this edition. Self-hosting is not a guarantee of security.

What you control

  • What to record: You choose which accounts, assets, and debts to include. Roofmere does not require you to connect a bank account to build your household overview.
  • What to share with AI: AI-ready export is intended for you to review and share deliberately with an AI service of your choice. Creating an export is not the same as automatically sending your financial information to an AI provider.
  • How to start: You can explore the product with fictional sample information before deciding whether to enter real data. See how to get started.

What we have built — and what that proves

The application repository includes automated test code for backup and restore, database schema rules, and cross-household access isolation. Having test code is useful engineering work, but it does not establish that a particular public release passed those tests. We will identify passing results by date and code revision when they are available and reviewed.

Independent checks and their limits

Public website configuration can be inspected using Mozilla Observatory, SecurityHeaders, and Qualys SSL Labs. These check HTTPS and browser-facing configuration, not whether a financial application correctly handles records, backups, or user isolation. We are not claiming a score from any of these services.

Verification status: Roofmere does not currently claim an independent security audit, SOC 2 certification, or endorsement by the services above. That is a limit on independently verified evidence, not a statement that the product is known to be insecure. Equally, the absence of a reported vulnerability is not proof of safety.

Practical precautions

  • Start with sample data if you are evaluating the product.
  • For Local, keep backups you can restore and protect your browser and device.
  • For Self-Hosted Cloud, review your authentication, access permissions, deployment settings, and recovery process before using sensitive records.
  • Never include passwords, API keys, or complete account numbers in support requests or AI exports.
