Use case 02 · Focused decision · Workflow to validate

Is a $325 annual-fee card worth it for my spending?

The question: Should I keep a rewards card, change products, or choose a different card based on my actual habits?

The data needed: Monthly eligible restaurant and grocery spending, plus relevant rewards categories, fees, credits actually used, redemption values and alternative-card terms.

Real walkthrough coming after validation

This case is a proposed analysis, not a recorded or verified Roofmere feature demonstration. Category-level transaction selection and export must be checked against the current product before a demo is published.

  1. Gather and review eligible spending by category; confirm Roofmere supports the required import and export detail.
  2. Share only the relevant, reviewed information with the AI service you choose.
  3. Compare annual net value under current issuer terms and realistic points redemption assumptions.
  4. Verify the math and any product-change eligibility directly with the card issuer before deciding.

Why Roofmere? A reusable, user-controlled financial record can reduce repeated manual preparation—if the required transaction detail is supported. For a one-off card analysis, an issuer CSV may be enough.

The $325 amount is an illustrative scenario, not a claim about today's issuer pricing. AI-generated comparisons may be inaccurate. Roofmere does not provide personalized financial advice.
