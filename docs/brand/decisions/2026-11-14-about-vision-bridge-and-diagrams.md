# About and Vision: a bridge between the pages, and two vision diagrams

Date: 2026-11-14 (America/Los_Angeles)
Owner: product owner
Status: Approved — the owner approved the exact wording and both diagrams in chat on 2026-11-14. Page titles and subtitles are unchanged.

## Why

About explains why Roofmere started; Vision explains where the founder believes things are going. The two pages did not link to each other, and Vision did not show the founder's core prediction: people will stop logging in to platform after platform; their AI agent will exchange data with APIs and other agents, so whoever controls the data matters most. Roofmere is the first, practical step toward that future, not its boundary.

## Vision — exact changes

- "Built for today. Designed with tomorrow in mind.", after "Roofmere starts with a practical financial organizer you can use today.": *Today, Roofmere already gives you control over what you share. Pro’s AI-ready Export lets you choose the information and level of detail before sharing it with an external AI tool. Nothing is sent automatically.*
- After the closing button: link *The story behind Roofmere →* (about.html).

### Diagram 1, after the opening question ("Who controls the personal information that makes AI useful?")

- Tag: Long-term vision
- Title: From logging in everywhere to asking your AI.
- Subtitle: I believe AI agents will exchange data with services directly. Then what matters most is who controls the data.
- Today: You → Log in → Bank app · Brokerage · Insurer. Caption: *Your information is spread across platforms, each with its own login and interface.*
- Tomorrow: You + your data ("you decide what to share, how much, with whom") → Your AI agent ⇄ Bank API · Other agents · Services. Caption: *Less switching between apps. Your AI connects with services on your behalf, using only the access you authorize.*

### Diagram 2, at the end of "You hold the keys."

- Tag: Long-term vision
- Title: The tools change. Your data and your keys stay with you.
- Subtitle: In the future, you decide what each AI or service can see, how much, and for what.
- Picture: "You + your data" as a house (records · documents · history); Any AI you choose, Local AI model, Apps & services, Advisor or family outside, each connected through a key.
- Today: You choose what Roofmere exports, and at what level of detail. Tomorrow: You choose what each tool can access.

## Alternatives considered

- One diagram with You → AI Agent → Data & Services: rejected; it placed the AI between the person and their data.
- Keys in Diagram 1: removed; without the house they had no meaning.
- "No logins" and "each one keeps you coming back": replaced with less absolute, more neutral captions.
- Deferring the diagrams until after the app launch: rejected by the owner; they carry the page's core idea.

## Not changed

Page titles and subtitles ("Why I Built Roofmere", "Your Personal Data Layer."), section order, the house-and-keys text, the disclaimers.
