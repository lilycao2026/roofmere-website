Why Roofmere exists

One account statement shows one balance. A mortgage statement shows a debt. A property estimate shows another part of the picture. Understanding a household's finances often means piecing together information scattered across tools and records.

Roofmere is being built to make that picture easier to organize, revisit and carry forward.

What we're building today

Roofmere is a Household Financial Organizer for accounts, investments, properties, debts and related context. It offers browser-local editions and a self-hosted cloud option with different capabilities and responsibilities. It is not a bank aggregator or a financial adviser.

Where we want to go

Tools will change. AI assistants will change. Your accumulated household knowledge should remain useful. Our long-term vision is a user-owned information foundation that can travel across compatible tools, without depending on a single AI platform.

That is a direction, not a claim that universal AI connections or an AI Data Layer are available today.

Our principle

Understand what you keep. Choose what you share. Be able to take a useful copy with you.
