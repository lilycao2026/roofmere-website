About Roofmere · A note from the founder

Why I Built Roofmere

Managing a household's finances shouldn't mean starting from scratch every time you need an answer.

I built Roofmere because I was tired of starting from scratch every time I needed to understand my household's finances.

Important information was scattered across investment accounts, mortgage documents, insurance policies, and spreadsheets. Even something as simple as confirming a mortgage rate or remembering a down payment meant searching through old records again.

As my family's financial life became more complex, I realized I didn't need another app to track every transaction. I needed a reliable place to keep the important details together, update what changed, and use that information whenever I needed it.

I'm a data solution architect by profession, and I believe good data systems should make information easier to understand, maintain, and use—not harder.

That belief became Roofmere.

I built it around a simple idea: your financial information should belong to you. You should be able to keep it organized, understand what you're working with, and decide how to use or share it.

Roofmere starts as a practical household financial organizer. My longer-term vision is to help people build a personal data foundation they control—one that can work with the tools and AI systems they choose, on their own terms.

Your information. Your choices. Your future.

Built independently. Grounded in real life.

Roofmere is an independently built product created by a data solution architect who wanted a better way to organize and use household financial information.

Roofmere is an organization tool, not a financial adviser. Connections to AI agents and other systems are part of a longer-term vision, not a promise of features available today.
