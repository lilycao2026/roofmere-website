# Roofmere website — contributor instructions

## Brand and positioning are locked
Before changing any website page, read [docs/brand/brand-governance.md](docs/brand/brand-governance.md).

Do not change approved slogan, tagline, page hero titles/subtitles, product positioning, target audience, core promises, edition positioning, or other approved strategic language unless the product owner has explicitly approved the exact change **and** a dated decision record has been added under `docs/brand/decisions/`.

Requests for video, UI, layout, CSS, SEO, accessibility, or ordinary content improvements do not authorize brand changes. Preserve existing approved copy verbatim. If the approved baseline is uncertain, ask before changing it. Check diffs before committing.

Supporting explanatory copy, layout, and video presentation can be improved within those constraints. Do not introduce unsupported product claims.

Do not trigger Netlify deployment or modify production databases as part of website-only work.
