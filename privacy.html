Privacy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version. Your financial records stay in your browser on your device, or in your own database for Private Cloud. Roofmere never uploads them: we do not receive or see them. This website counts visits without cookies and does not track you as a person. We do not sell data or show ads.

Who we are

Roofmere is built and run by Lily Cao, an independent developer ("we"). Contact: lilycao2026@gmail.com. The rules for using Roofmere are in the Terms of Use.

This website (roofmere.com)

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps technical logs of requests, such as IP addresses, for security, under its own privacy terms.
  • Visit counts. Some pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, pages viewed, the site you came from, country and device type. It uses no cookies and does not identify or follow individual visitors.
  • No forms, no accounts. The website has no sign-up, no forms and no advertising or tracking cookies. If you e-mail us, we receive your message and e-mail address and use them only to answer you.

The Roofmere app

The app keeps your accounts, balances, properties, transactions, notes and files in your browser's storage on your device. It does not upload them. We only receive what you send on purpose: a purchase (through our store, Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad, as seller of record), a licence check, a feedback or support message, a refund request, or answers to an optional survey. If you clear the browser's data, the records are gone unless you have a backup file.

The app's full privacy notice lists exactly what is received, why, who processes it for us and how long it is kept. It opens from Privacy at the bottom of the app's home page, and is also at roofmere.netlify.app/privacy.html.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to lilycao2026@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, including the rights to access, delete and object under the EU and UK GDPR and the rights to know and delete under California law. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Roofmere is meant for adults managing household finances, and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change how this website or the app handles data, we update this page and its date.
