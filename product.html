Three things Roofmere helps you do

Assemble

Organize accounts, investments, properties and debts. Manually add accounts and properties, update balances, or import supported Fidelity Full View and Positions files in both Free and Pro.

Review

See assets and liabilities together. Keep property values alongside associated mortgage balances and revisit your figures when they change.

Reuse

Save backups, export supported records and prepare a reviewable financial snapshot for a compatible AI tool.

A financial review, not a daily spending chore.

Roofmere does not connect to your bank or update balances automatically. Local Free includes unlimited accounts and properties, the full net-worth dashboard, supported app imports, and backup/restore. Local Pro adds screenshot recognition, custom Excel/CSV mapping, cash-flow transaction imports, history comparisons and reports. You decide when to refresh your household picture. You do not need to categorize every purchase.

Review your financial snapshot before sharing it with AI.

Choose relevant information, review the export and copy it into the AI service you prefer. Roofmere does not send it directly. The external AI provider processes anything you paste under its own terms; verify important calculations and conclusions.

Know where your information lives.

Local editions store financial records in the current browser and do not sync between devices. Private Cloud uses infrastructure and a database you configure and maintain. Backup and setup responsibilities differ.
