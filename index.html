Private by design

Two ways to keep your finances in your hands.

Local Free & Local Pro

Your financial data stays in your browser.

Your financial records are stored locally on your device, not in a Roofmere-operated financial database. No bank connection, cloud account or financial-data upload is required for Local. You control what you export and share. Each browser is independent, so your phone and laptop do not sync automatically.

Your responsibility: download backups regularly. Clearing browser data, changing devices, or storage cleanup can remove local records. Offline use is a planned capability, not yet a verified launch promise.

Private Cloud

Your own website, database and access rules

Set up Roofmere using your own hosting and database accounts. You control your deployed site, can inspect your database, and can invite authorized family members to use the same household dashboard across devices.

If you are comfortable with code, you can customize the frontend, backend functions and database schema included in your deployment. You also take responsibility for setup, access security, hosting costs, updates and backups.

What we mean by “your data”: Roofmere does not require bank logins or a transfer of your financial records into a Roofmere-operated financial database. Local financial records stay in your browser; Private Cloud records stay in your own configured database. This is a data-control design, not a guarantee that any internet-connected software is absolutely secure. Pro licensing may use a small authorization service for email, purchase and activation details — never account balances or financial documents. This website (roofmere.com, not the Roofmere app) counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors.
Software architecture

Your data. Your infrastructure.

See where your financial records live and how Roofmere works. Local runs in your browser; Private Cloud runs with your own Netlify and Supabase accounts.

Local Free / Local Pro: financial records stay in the current browser’s IndexedDB. Keep manual backups; browsers and devices do not sync automatically. Neither Local edition includes a file Vault.

Private Cloud: your Netlify Functions handle authorized requests, while your Supabase project stores financial records and private documents. Authorized family members access the same household data across devices.

Local Pro licensing and installable offline use remain planned. Pro Demo stays available with fictional sample data. AI-ready Export prepares data and prompts that you choose to share with an external AI.

Questions, answered.

Are Local Free and Local Pro separate websites?

No. Local Free and Local Pro use the same browser-local records. A paid Pro license unlocks additional features without transferring your records; the permanent Pro Demo uses separate fictional data.

Can I try Pro before buying?

Yes. Pro Demo is permanently available with preset fictional data to explore implemented Pro features. Local Pro is a one-time purchase ($39 beta / $49 full release); refund requests are accepted within 30 days under the existing policy (reason required).

Does Local sync across devices?

No. Each browser has its own data. Use backups to move data between devices. Private Cloud is intended for shared household access and syncing.

Does Roofmere send my financial data to AI?

No automatic AI upload is required. AI-ready export prepares information that you choose whether, where and how to share with an AI assistant. Review exported content before sharing sensitive information.

Which edition includes Document Vault?

Private Cloud includes file storage in your own configured cloud. Local Free and Local Pro do not include file uploads; Local Pro keeps text notes, and both export backups.

Does Roofmere connect to my bank?

No bank connection is required. You enter and review financial information yourself.

Is it a subscription?

The planned paid editions use one-time purchases. The existing self-hosted beta has its own current checkout terms; third-party hosting may have separate charges.

Is the current Gumroad download the new Local Pro?

No. The current Gumroad product is the existing self-hosted beta. Local Pro checkout and licensing are not yet confirmed live; the permanent Pro Demo is separate from the current self-hosted beta.

Does Roofmere provide financial or legal advice?

No. Roofmere is an organization and planning tool, not personalized financial or legal advice.
