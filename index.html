Designed for ownership

Your family's financial information belongs to your family.

Roofmere is a self-hosted software template. You deploy it with your own Supabase and Netlify accounts, instead of giving Roofmere custody of your financial records.

It does not require linking bank accounts or granting access to your financial institutions. You control the information you enter and maintain your own backups.

What self-hosted means

01 · Your database
Financial records live in your Supabase project.
02 · Your deployment
Your app runs through your Netlify site.
03 · Your responsibility

Questions, answered.

Is Roofmere a subscription?

Roofmere is sold as a self-hosted template, not a hosted Roofmere subscription. Supabase and Netlify may charge separately depending on usage and plan.

Does Roofmere connect to my bank?

No bank connection is required. You enter and import financial information yourself.

Do I need to install anything?

Yes. You create your own Supabase and Netlify projects and follow the setup instructions. Some configuration steps are technical.

Will my dashboard start with example balances?

No. Your household starts empty so you can add your own financial information.

Can multiple family members use it?

The template is designed for one household per deployment, with permitted family member logins.

Does it provide financial or legal advice?

No. Roofmere is an organization and planning tool. Estate-planning drafts are examples, not legal advice.
